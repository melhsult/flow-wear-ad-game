# Case: Advertising Game (English summary)

*Translated from the original Danish brief, available as [`case-brief-da.pdf`](case-brief-da.pdf).*

We were hired by **Flow Wear**, a company that makes clothing with built-in electronics to measure the user's movements, posture, heart rate, hydration and more.

## Requirements

1. Develop an **advertising game** that engages the company's customers and creates awareness around the company and its products.
2. The company's primary target group has so far been **young athletes**, but they are open to expanding into other areas if possible.
3. The game will be **published on the company's website and on their social media pages**.

## How our game responds to the brief

| Requirement | Our solution |
|---|---|
| Engage customers | Fast, arcade-style gameplay that is easy to pick up and replay |
| Brand awareness | Logo on the balloon and a Flow Wear banner flown by a plane |
| Young athletes | Reaction-based gameplay with quick controls |
| Broader audience | Simple keyboard controls and no text-heavy rules |
| Web and social media | Scratch projects can be embedded on websites and shared as links |
